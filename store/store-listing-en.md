# Product name
Everstep

# Short description
Stay focused and finish your work with Pomodoro sessions, tasks, ambient sounds, and clear productivity insights.

# Description
Everstep is a modern Pomodoro and focus app designed to simplify your work routine. Start the timer, select the task you are working on, and follow your progress day by day.

Create your own rhythm with customizable focus, short-break, and long-break durations. Plan tasks with categories, priorities, notes, and estimated Pomodoro counts, then see how each completed session moves the task forward.

Weekly charts, a 12-week focus calendar, daily goals, focus score, and streak tracking make your habits visible. White, pink, and brown noise, together with bundled rain, cafe, thunderstorm, wind, ocean, forest, and fireplace recordings, all work offline.

Everstep requires no account, displays no ads, and sends no usage data to a server. Your tasks, settings, and session history remain on your computer. You can export a JSON backup, CSV report, or printable PDF report whenever you need it.

# Product features
Custom focus, short-break, and long-break durations
Tasks with categories, priorities, notes, and Pomodoro estimates
Daily goals, focus score, and streak tracking
Weekly charts and a 12-week activity heatmap
Six themes with a custom accent color
Offline natural ambience plus white, pink, and brown noise
Desktop notifications and system tray integration
JSON backup plus CSV and PDF reporting
Keyboard shortcuts and distraction-free focus mode
Turkish, English, Spanish, Japanese, German, Italian, and Azerbaijani interface

No account, ads, analytics, or cloud tracking
