# Everstep Ambient Audio

Everstep release builds include seven natural ambience recordings:

- Natural rain
- Cafe ambience
- Thunderstorm
- Wind and rustling leaves
- Ocean waves
- Forest and birds
- Fireplace and campfire

The files are bundled under `public/audio` and play fully offline. Their creators, source pages, and license are recorded in [AUDIO_CREDITS.md](AUDIO_CREDITS.md).

The raw recordings are kept out of the public Git repository because the Pixabay Content License does not allow distributing unchanged content on a standalone basis. This does not affect the Microsoft Store package, where the recordings are part of the application experience.

White, pink, and brown noise do not use recordings. Everstep generates them locally with the Web Audio API.
